## Problem Statement

The live `/book-now` page advertises four offers through disconnected Paystack payment links. After payment, customers must find a separate confirmation page, re-enter receipt information, and then choose a slot. Salim needs one trustworthy flow: choose a 30- or 60-minute session, pay, return automatically to the calendar, claim one available time without double booking, and receive everything needed to attend or manage the appointment.

## Demand Evidence

- Eng. Mathias specified the required journey: “when someone clicks book now, is taken to choice the different sessions 30 minutes, or 1 hour. then he is taken to pay. after which he is taken to the calendar to pick a date and time.”
- The site is already live with Paystack links, a signed webhook, availability administration, booking records, customer dashboards, and CRM follow-up. This is an operating workflow upgrade, not a speculative new product.
- No unassisted customer observation exists yet. The release must establish a measurable baseline instead of claiming a conversion improvement without evidence.

## Status Quo

1. `/book-now` displays 30-minute, 60-minute, 120-minute, and multiweek offers.
2. Each offer opens a static Paystack payment page in a new tab.
3. A signed `charge.success` webhook creates or updates a paid `Booking` and CRM transaction.
4. The customer must independently reach `/book-now/confirm`, then manually enter name, email, offer, and Paystack reference.
5. `/api/booking/confirm` verifies the webhook-created payment and atomically marks an explicit `AvailabilitySlot` booked.
6. Salim manages individual availability slots and sees confirmed bookings in the existing dashboard and CRM.

The security foundation is useful, but the customer journey loses continuity at the payment boundary and has no durable recovery link, integrated confirmation, rescheduling, cancellation, calendar export, or funnel trace.

## Target User and Narrowest Wedge

The primary user is a prospective coaching client ready to pay for one private conversation with Salim. The first production release supports exactly two one-on-one services:

- Focused Session: 30 minutes
- Clarity Session: 60 minutes

The wedge is a completed paid appointment in one session: service choice → identity → secure Paystack checkout → verified return → timezone-aware calendar → atomic reservation → confirmation. The system must also recover when the browser closes after payment.

## Constraints

- Keep payment card and mobile-money details on Paystack.
- Use the existing Next.js 14 application, PostgreSQL database, Prisma client, authentication, CRM, Paystack webhook, and Salim admin dashboard.
- Do not add Calendly, Cal.com, or another scheduling vendor.
- Store instants in UTC and show the customer’s selected IANA timezone, defaulting from the browser.
- Never treat a callback visit as proof of payment.
- Never allow application timing alone to enforce overlap prevention; PostgreSQL must reject conflicting reservations.
- All policy values must be admin-configurable. Seed safe defaults instead of hard-coding permanent business rules.
- Preserve historical 120-minute and private-coaching records, but remove those products from the public instant-booking path.

## Research Findings

- Paystack’s current guidance requires server-side checkout initialization and verification of transaction status and amount before delivering value. The redirect returns a transaction reference, while signed webhooks provide recovery when the customer never returns: <https://paystack.com/docs/payments/accept-payments/>, <https://paystack.com/docs/payments/verify-payments/>, <https://paystack.com/docs/payments/webhooks/>.
- Established scheduling products treat duration, location, availability, minimum notice, buffers, date range, timezone, connected-calendar conflicts, reminders, rescheduling, cancellation, and no-show tracking as core workflow behavior. The evidence came from Calendly’s current event-type, availability-troubleshooting, workflow, and no-show guidance in the [Calendly Help Center](https://help.calendly.com/).

The conventional sequence is sound here. The project’s existing split flow is not a special case that benefits from manual receipt entry.

## Approved Premises

1. Public instant booking contains only 30- and 60-minute sessions.
2. Customer identity is captured before payment.
3. Checkout is initialized server-side with a first-party callback URL.
4. Payment status, currency, amount, email, reference, and service are verified server-side.
5. Calendar availability is shown in the customer’s timezone and stored in UTC.
6. PostgreSQL is the final authority preventing overlapping reservations.
7. Salim controls working hours, blocked dates, minimum notice, booking horizon, and buffers.
8. Customers receive a receipt, preparation guidance, calendar file, and secure management links.
9. Every expected failure has an explicit recovery path.
10. The funnel emits privacy-conscious, step-level operational events.

## Approaches Considered

- Retrofit existing pages: rejected because it leaves booking state distributed across static links, callback parameters, and manual confirmation.
- Native booking state machine: approved because it provides one owned workflow and makes every transition observable and recoverable.
- External scheduler: rejected because it splits control and contradicts the project’s self-hosted direction.

## Recommended Architecture

### Customer flow

`/book-now` becomes a focused three-stage booking application inside the existing site shell:

1. **Choose and identify**
   - Compare 30- and 60-minute services with duration, result, meeting format, price, and cancellation summary.
   - Collect full name and email. Phone and coaching context are optional and must state their purpose.
   - Create a booking checkout on the server and redirect the browser to the returned Paystack authorization URL.
2. **Pay and recover**
   - Paystack returns to `/book-now/schedule?reference=...`.
   - The server verifies the transaction directly. The signed webhook remains authoritative and idempotent.
   - Either verification path advances the same booking once. A webhook-created recovery email lets a paid customer resume scheduling if the original browser never returns.
3. **Schedule and confirm**
   - Show the verified service and payment, selected timezone, month/day controls, and valid start times.
   - Recheck availability when the customer confirms.
   - Insert the reservation in one transaction. A database exclusion constraint rejects overlap even under concurrent requests.
   - Show confirmation, preparation guidance, receipt reference, calendar download, and manage/reschedule controls.

The approved rough wireframe is `/tmp/gstack-booking-wireframe.png` for this session. Production design uses the existing navy, cream, gold, Playfair Display, and Inter system, with restrained transitions and a single primary action per step.

### Booking and payment state machines

Payment and appointment state are separate. Paystack’s server response is the payment authority; the callback and signed webhook are merely idempotent triggers into the same `reconcilePayment(reference)` operation.

| State field | Value | Allowed next values | Guard and side effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `paymentStatus` | `checkout_started` | `succeeded`, `failed`, `abandoned` | Checkout reference and immutable price snapshot exist. |
| `paymentStatus` | `failed` | `checkout_started` on a new reference | Old attempt stays in the event history. |
| `paymentStatus` | `abandoned` | `checkout_started` on a new reference | Set only after expiry; never inferred while Paystack still reports a pending state. |
| `paymentStatus` | `succeeded` | `refunded`, `partially_refunded` | Requires verified reference, status, amount, currency, payer, and service metadata; enqueue recovery after the grace period if unscheduled. |
| `paymentStatus` | `partially_refunded` | `refunded` | Provider reconciliation owns refunded totals. Appointment policy determines whether the time remains occupied. |
| `paymentStatus` | `refunded` | terminal | A full refund cancels a future appointment and releases occupancy in the same transaction. |
| `appointmentStatus` | `not_ready` | `unscheduled` | Initial value; advances only with verified successful payment. |
| `appointmentStatus` | `unscheduled` | `scheduled`, `expired_unscheduled` | Requires successful payment. |
| `appointmentStatus` | `scheduled` | `cancelled`, `completed`, `no_show` | Rescheduling is an atomic reservation replacement plus a `booking_rescheduled` event, not a state. |
| `appointmentStatus` | `cancelled` | terminal | Release reservation; store reason and refund status separately. |
| `appointmentStatus` | `completed` | terminal | May be set only after scheduled end. |
| `appointmentStatus` | `no_show` | terminal | May be set only after scheduled start. |
| `appointmentStatus` | `expired_unscheduled` | `unscheduled` by admin recovery | Payment remains visible and requires human resolution; it is not silently forfeited. |

Before payment, `appointmentStatus=not_ready`. Successful payment reconciliation atomically changes `paymentStatus` to `succeeded` and `appointmentStatus` to `unscheduled`. No other payment transition changes appointment state except a full refund of a future appointment, which atomically cancels it and releases occupancy.

Routes request transitions through `src/services/bookings/lifecycle.ts`; they never update state fields directly. Each transition uses optimistic versioning, appends one `BookingEvent`, and deduplicates side effects.

### Data model

Evolve the existing service without discarding historical records:

- `BookingService`
  - `id`, `slug`, `name`, `description`, `durationMinutes`, `priceMinor`, `currency`, `meetingFormat`, `isPublic`, `isActive`, `sortOrder`, timestamps.
  - Seed 30- and 60-minute services from the current Paystack products after confirming their production amounts.
- `Booking`
  - Retain existing IDs, account relation, customer identity, notes, CRM relation, legacy payment reference for compatibility, and timestamps. New provider references live only on payment attempts.
  - Add `serviceId`, `durationMinutesSnapshot`, `amountMinor`, `currency`, `customerTimezone`, `paymentStatus`, `appointmentStatus`, `statusReason`, `paidAt`, `cancelledAt`, `completedAt`, `noShowAt`, `rescheduleCount`, `cancellationPolicySnapshot`, `reschedulePolicySnapshot`, `version`, `updatedAt`.
  - Snapshot duration, amount, and currency so later service edits never rewrite purchase history.
- `BookingPaymentAttempt`
  - `id`, `bookingId`, provider, globally unique provider reference, immutable payer email/service/amount/currency snapshots, status, `refundedAmountMinor`, `refundStatus`, authorization expiry, provider timestamps, verified payload digest, timestamps.
  - Every retry creates a new attempt. A late webhook reconciles by its attempt reference and can still advance the owning booking. If more than one valid attempt succeeds, create an admin refund-review task rather than silently crediting twice.
  - The first verified successful attempt becomes `creditedPaymentAttemptId` on `Booking`; booking-level `paidAt`, amount, currency, and compatibility reference derive only from it. Later successful attempts never replace it. Attempt refund status is canonical: booking `paymentStatus=succeeded` while credited refund is `none|pending|failed`, `partially_refunded` when credited refund is `partial`, and `refunded` when it is `full`.
- `BookingReservation`
  - `id`, unique `bookingId`, non-null `startAt`, `endAt`, `occupiedStartAt`, `occupiedEndAt`, `status`, timestamps. The raw migration also sets all four timestamps `NOT NULL` before adding range constraints.
  - Occupancy-holding statuses are exactly `active` and `rescheduling`. Cancelled/released reservations do not occupy time.
- `BookingSession`
  - `id`, `bookingId`, `tokenHash`, `expiresAt`, `lastSeenAt`, `revokedAt`, timestamps.
  - The browser receives only a random opaque token in a `Secure`, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, path-scoped cookie. Rotate it after payment and scheduling; store only SHA-256 hashes; default expiry is 24 hours before payment and 30 days after payment.
- `BookingAccessToken`
  - `id`, `bookingId`, purpose (`schedule_recovery`, `manage`, `email_verify`), `tokenHash`, `expiresAt`, `consumedAt`, `revokedAt`, timestamps.
  - Email URLs carry a single-use random token. The first valid request exchanges it for a scoped HttpOnly session and redirects immediately to a token-free URL. Responses set `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, request logging redacts token paths, and resend revokes unused tokens of the same purpose.
- `IdempotencyRecord`
  - `operation`, `principalHash`, `keyHash`, `requestHash`, `status`, `responseStatus`, `responseBody`, `expiresAt`, timestamps.
  - Unique on `(operation, principalHash, keyHash)`. Identical replays return the stored response; a reused key with a different request hash returns `409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`. Processing leases expire after two minutes; completed checkout records live 24 hours and schedule/manage records 7 days.
- `AvailabilityRule`
  - Recurring weekday windows in Salim’s IANA timezone, effective date range, and active flag.
- `AvailabilityException`
  - Closed or added windows for travel, holidays, and one-off changes.
- `BookingPolicy`
  - Singleton configuration: Salim timezone, slot interval, minimum notice, booking horizon, pre/post buffer, cancellation cutoff, reschedule cutoff, maximum reschedules, support channel, and meeting instructions.
- `BookingEvent`
  - Append-only operational trace: booking ID, event type, source, safe metadata, actor ID where applicable, and timestamp.
- `NotificationOutbox`
  - Durable email jobs with template, payload, dedupe key, attempts, lease owner/expiry, next attempt, sent time, dead-letter time, and last error. Payment and booking transactions enqueue messages; delivery happens outside those transactions.

Use a raw Prisma migration because Prisma schema syntax cannot express the final overlap guarantee:

```sql
CREATE EXTENSION IF NOT EXISTS btree_gist;
ALTER TABLE "BookingReservation"
  ADD CONSTRAINT "BookingReservation_no_active_overlap"
  EXCLUDE USING gist (
    tstzrange("occupiedStartAt", "occupiedEndAt", '[)') WITH &&
  ) WHERE ("status" IN ('active', 'rescheduling'));
ALTER TABLE "BookingReservation"
  ADD CONSTRAINT "BookingReservation_time_order"
  CHECK ("occupiedStartAt" < "startAt" OR "occupiedStartAt" = "startAt");
ALTER TABLE "BookingReservation"
  ADD CONSTRAINT "BookingReservation_end_order"
  CHECK ("startAt" < "endAt" AND "endAt" <= "occupiedEndAt");
```

The occupied range includes configured buffers. A cancellation/full refund changes reservation status in the same transaction that changes appointment state. Rescheduling atomically updates the existing reservation range; if the exclusion constraint rejects it, rollback preserves the old time. The previous range stays in `booking_rescheduled` event metadata. Serialization failures receive at most two bounded retries with jitter; exclusion conflicts are not retried and return a slot-conflict response.

### Legacy migration and backfill

The release is additive and uses this deterministic mapping:

| Legacy data | New representation |
| --- | --- |
| `Booking.status = paid` | `paymentStatus=succeeded`, `appointmentStatus=unscheduled` |
| `Booking.status = confirmed` with booked slot | `paymentStatus=succeeded`, `appointmentStatus=scheduled`; reservation uses slot start/duration and zero legacy buffer |
| `Booking.status = pending` | `paymentStatus=checkout_started`, `appointmentStatus=not_ready`, flagged `legacy_review_required` |
| `offerName` matches 30/60 product | Link seeded service and snapshot its current duration/verified amount |
| Historical 120-minute/private offer | Create inactive, non-public service for reporting and link by name |
| Unknown offer or missing amount | Keep nullable service, preserve text, flag admin review; never invent an amount |
| `slotId` | Retain during compatibility window; backfill one reservation, validate, then switch reads to reservation. Do not drop in this release. |
| `amountKobo` | Copy to `amountMinor`; “minor” means the ISO currency minor unit. Retain old field during compatibility window. |

Before migration: database backup plus restore rehearsal. After backfill: assert booking counts unchanged, every legacy confirmed booking has one reservation, no paid reference duplicates exist, reservation ranges do not overlap, and all mapped totals match. Rollback is application-only after writes begin: the additive schema remains, and the old release may run only while dual-write compatibility is enabled.

### API contracts

All JSON errors use `{ "error": { "code": string, "message": string, "correlationId": string, "retryable": boolean, "fieldErrors"?: object } }`. List responses use cursor pagination. Availability accepts at most 31 days and never exceeds the configured horizon. Private responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`; public services may use a five-minute cache with revalidation.

- `GET /api/booking/services`
  - Returns only active public services. Prices come from the server, never browser-submitted values.
- `POST /api/booking/checkout`
  - Accepts service slug, name, normalized email, optional phone/context, consent fields, timezone, and an idempotency key.
  - The page first issues a random `booking_checkout_principal` SameSite cookie. Its hash is the unauthenticated idempotency principal; email and IP are never principal keys.
  - Creates the booking session, `checkout_started` booking, and first payment attempt, then initializes Paystack with server-owned amount/currency/metadata/callback URL and returns `201 { bookingId, authorizationUrl, expiresAt }`.
  - Returns `409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`, `422 SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, or `502 PAYMENT_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` using the shared envelope.
- `GET /api/booking/payment-status?reference=...`
  - Verifies ownership through an HttpOnly signed booking-session cookie, then returns a safe state. Direct Paystack verification repairs delayed webhooks.
- `GET /api/booking/availability?reference=...&from=...&to=...&timezone=...`
  - Requires a paid, unscheduled booking. Computes candidates from rules/exceptions, policy, service duration, buffers, and active reservations.
- `POST /api/booking/schedule`
  - Requires the paid booking session, expected booking version, chosen UTC start, and idempotency key.
  - Recomputes validity and inserts the protected reservation in one transaction. Returns `201` on first success, the stored `201` on identical replay, `409 SLOT_CONFLICT` for overlap, `409 BOOKING_VERSION_CONFLICT` for stale state, or `422 SLOT_OUTSIDE_POLICY`.
- `GET /api/booking/access/[token]`
  - Consumes the single-use token, creates a scoped HttpOnly management session, and redirects to token-free `/book-now/manage`.
- `GET /api/booking/manage`
  - Requires the management session and returns safe booking details.
- `POST /api/booking/manage/reschedule` and `/api/booking/manage/cancel`
  - Require the management session, enforce policy, update transactionally, append an event, and enqueue notices. Tokens never appear in mutation URLs.
- `GET /api/booking/[id]/calendar.ics`
  - Requires the booking session, signed-in owner, admin, or management token.
- Existing `/api/payments/webhook`
  - Continues raw-body HMAC-SHA512 verification and routes deterministically on signed metadata `purpose=booking|donation`.
  - Booking events reconcile through `BookingPaymentAttempt`; donation events retain the mission-support path. Unknown purpose is quarantined for admin review and never treated as a booking.
  - During compatibility, route known legacy payloads before quarantine: a reference already present on `Booking` is booking; known mission-support metadata is donation; legacy `offer_name` matching the four historical coaching offers is booking. Record `legacy_webhook_routed`, preserve the original offer text, and test fixtures for every legacy shape. Newly initialized transactions must carry `metadataVersion=2` and explicit purpose.

Every mutation validates with Zod, normalizes email consistently, applies request-size limits, and emits a correlation ID. Logs contain booking/reference IDs but never management tokens or full Paystack payloads.

### Authorization matrix

| Capability | Booking-session cookie | Signed-in matching owner | Management token | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read payment state / schedule initial slot | Yes | Yes | Yes after paid recovery | Yes |
| Read full booking | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Download ICS | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Reschedule/cancel | Yes, subject to policy | Yes, subject to policy | Yes, subject to policy | Yes, with audited override |
| Edit service/policy/availability | No | No | No | Yes |
| Mark complete/no-show/refund state | No | No | No | Yes |

If multiple credentials exist, the most restrictive customer policy still applies unless an admin explicitly uses an audited override. Account linking occurs only when normalized signed-in email equals verified payer email; otherwise the booking remains token/session-managed.

### Availability algorithm

1. Resolve Salim’s recurring windows and exceptions in the configured IANA timezone.
2. Convert candidate instants to UTC across daylight-saving boundaries.
3. Generate starts at the configured interval within the booking horizon.
4. Remove starts inside minimum notice, outside the service-fitting window, or overlapping active occupied ranges.
5. Return display labels plus UTC instants; never accept a browser-computed end time.
6. On scheduling, recompute all rules and rely on the database exclusion constraint as the final concurrency gate.

Exception precedence is: highest explicit priority wins, then the most specific range, then closed wins a tie; recurring rules are lowest priority. A full-day holiday closure blocks the day unless Salim adds a higher-priority two-hour opening. Cross-midnight rules split at local midnight before UTC conversion. Ambiguous daylight-saving starts choose the earlier offset and display it; nonexistent local starts are omitted. Purchase price/duration snapshots never change. Availability policy is evaluated at scheduling; cancellation/reschedule policy is snapshotted on first reservation. Rescheduling holds the old time until the replacement update commits.

Admin diagnostics must explain why a time is unavailable: outside working hours, blocked date, minimum notice, buffer, existing booking, past time, or booking horizon.

### Customer recovery and communication

- Checkout initialization failure: preserve entered details and provide retry plus WhatsApp support.
- Abandoned/failed payment: show a neutral payment state and allow a fresh server-generated reference.
- Callback before webhook: verify directly with Paystack, then advance idempotently.
- Webhook without callback: send a signed scheduling-recovery link.
- Paid but unscheduled: show “payment safe, scheduling incomplete” in the customer dashboard and admin queue.
- Slot race: retain payment and selection context, refresh slots, and announce the conflict accessibly.
- No availability: keep payment safe, offer a notification/request-time path, and create a high-priority CRM task.
- Expired management link: verify by email through a new short-lived link; never reveal whether arbitrary email addresses have bookings.
- Email delivery failure: booking remains valid; outbox retries and admin diagnostics expose the failure.

Email templates: payment received/schedule now, booking confirmed, rescheduled, cancelled, reminder, and internal Salim notification. Confirmation and reminder include the timezone, meeting instructions, preparation prompts, support route, and calendar attachment/link.

### Worker and retry ownership

Add `services/booking-worker/` as one singleton PM2 fork process named `salimcyrus-booking-worker`, with its own README, tests, evals, heartbeat, maximum database pool of three, and graceful SIGTERM that stops claims and lets the current lease finish. Duplicate processes remain safe because jobs use PostgreSQL `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, 60-second leases, and deterministic dedupe keys. PM2 restarts it with `pm2 restart salimcyrus-booking-worker --update-env`; lease expiry recovers interrupted work. Email retries at 1, 5, 30, and 120 minutes; after five failures it dead-letters the job, emits `booking_notification_failed`, and creates one high-priority CRM task. A five-minute job expires abandoned checkouts and a 15-minute job identifies paid-unscheduled bookings; reminders are enqueued 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment. Alerts fire when heartbeat is older than two minutes, the oldest pending job exceeds 10 minutes, or any dead-letter exists.

Recovery notices use dedupe key `schedule-recovery:{bookingId}` and a 10-minute delay. Successful scheduling before delivery cancels that pending notice. The worker never changes a payment to successful without `reconcilePayment` receiving a verified Paystack response.

Refund state is reconciled from Paystack into `refundedAmountMinor` and `refundStatus` (`none`, `pending`, `partial`, `full`, `failed`). Cancellation does not imply refund. A full refund of a future appointment atomically cancels and releases its reservation; partial or failed refunds keep appointment state unchanged for admin review.

### Salim administration

Extend the existing admin area with:

- Services: activate, hide, reorder, price, description, duration, and meeting format.
- Availability: weekly schedule, exceptions, minimum notice, horizon, slot interval, buffers, and timezone preview.
- Bookings: filters for paid-unscheduled, upcoming, completed, cancelled, refunded, and no-show; safe manual reschedule/cancel; customer/contact link; payment and event timeline.
- Diagnostics: explain unavailable slots, webhook/payment mismatches, failed notifications, and bookings needing human follow-up.
- Audit: record every admin policy, service, availability, and booking mutation with actor and before/after metadata.

Policy timing is explicit:

| Setting | Existing checkout | Paid but unscheduled | Scheduled booking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price/currency/duration | Immutable purchase snapshot | Immutable | Immutable |
| Working hours/exceptions/horizon/minimum notice | Evaluate when scheduling | Evaluate when scheduling | Never invalidates booked time |

### Privacy, security, and integrity

- Paystack secret stays server-only; card data never enters the application.
- Verify webhook signatures against the raw request body before parsing or writing.
- Verify transaction `status`, `reference`, `amount`, `currency`, normalized payer email, and service metadata.
- Use cryptographically random, single-booking management tokens; store only hashes; support rotation and expiry.
- Rate-limit checkout, payment verification, availability, management-link requests, and confirmation endpoints by appropriate identity/IP keys.
- Avoid email/account enumeration and redact personal data from logs and analytics.
- Use CSRF protection or same-site signed sessions for browser mutations.
- Add database constraints for unique payment-attempt reference, one reservation row per booking, valid time ordering, nonnegative amounts, and no overlapping active occupied ranges.
- Back up the production database before migration and use an additive, backward-compatible migration. Do not drop legacy columns in this release.

### Observability

Emit structured events for:

- `booking_service_viewed`
- `booking_service_selected`
- `booking_checkout_started`
- `booking_payment_succeeded`
- `booking_calendar_viewed`
- `booking_slot_selected`
- `booking_scheduled`
- `booking_rescheduled`
- `booking_cancelled`
- `booking_recovery_sent`
- `booking_notification_failed`

The operational dashboard reports counts and conversion between selected → checkout started → paid → scheduled. The first release establishes baseline rates; no improvement claim is valid before live observations.

`BookingEvent.type` covers every lifecycle transition: checkout started/retried, payment succeeded/failed/abandoned, refund pending/partial/full/failed, schedule confirmed/rescheduled/cancelled/expired/recovered/completed/no-show, recovery queued/sent, and notification failed. `source` is `customer_web`, `paystack_callback`, `paystack_webhook`, `worker`, or `admin`; `actorId` is nullable. Versioned metadata is limited to service slug, transition, timezone, safe failure code, prior/new UTC range, and correlation ID.

Pre-checkout views and selections use a separate `BookingFunnelEvent` with random 24-hour `funnelSessionId`, event type, service slug, and timestamp only. Checkout attaches that ID to the booking. Funnel queries count distinct session IDs before checkout and booking IDs afterward within a stated UTC window; they never use email or IP. Dashboards link to exact queries and name `services/bookings/README.md` as operational owner.

### Atomic transition effects

| Transition | Same-transaction writes | Dedupe key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checkout retry | New payment attempt and booking event | Idempotency record |
| Payment success | Attempt, booking states, event, delayed recovery outbox, CRM transaction | Provider reference and `payment-success:{attemptId}` |
| Initial schedule | Reservation, policy snapshots/version, event, confirmation outbox, CRM activity/task | `schedule:{bookingId}:{version}` |
| Reschedule | Reservation update, version/count, event, notice outbox, CRM activity | `reschedule:{bookingId}:{version}` |
| Cancel | Release reservation, booking state/version, event, notice outbox, CRM activity/task | `cancel:{bookingId}:{version}` |
| Full refund | Refund totals, release reservation, both states, event, outbox, CRM task | Provider refund ID |
| Complete/no-show/recover | Booking state/version, event, CRM activity/task as applicable | `{transition}:{bookingId}:{version}` |

## Delivery Plan and Release Gates

Each gate is independently deployable behind `BOOKING_V2_ENABLED`; admin routes use `BOOKING_V2_ADMIN_ENABLED`. General availability occurs only after all five gates pass, but internal testing begins after Gate 2.

1. **Gate 1: contracts and migration**
   - Add booking lifecycle types, service/policy/rule/exception/event/outbox tables, additive booking fields, constraints, and seed data.
   - Add migration rollback notes and a production backup/restore rehearsal.
   - Acceptance: verify `btree_gist` exists or the production role can create it; migration applies to a production-shaped copy, backfill queries are clean, legacy reads are unchanged, and rollback is rehearsed. If the app role lacks privilege, an operator installs the extension first; application-only overlap checks are not an allowed fallback.
2. **Gate 2: paid scheduling core**
   - Centralize transitions, Paystack reconciliation, availability calculation, reservation, management tokens, ICS output, and outbox enqueueing under `src/services/bookings/`.
   - Add the API routes, adapt the webhook, and build the responsive choose → pay → schedule → confirm path.
   - Acceptance: two Paystack test purchases complete end-to-end, callback/webhook races remain idempotent, and overlapping requests yield one reservation.
3. **Gate 3: recovery and notifications**
   - Ship the worker, outbox, paid-unscheduled recovery, confirmation, ICS, reminders, and failure diagnostics.
   - Acceptance: closing the callback browser still delivers one recovery link; retries/dead-letter behavior is observable.
4. **Gate 4: customer management**
   - Ship secure manage, reschedule, and cancellation flows with policy snapshots and audit events.
   - Acceptance: token/auth matrix tests pass; atomic reschedule never drops the old slot on failure.
5. **Gate 5: Salim administration and launch**
   - Ship services, policy, recurring availability, exceptions, booking operations, no-show/completion, diagnostics, and funnel reports.
   - Acceptance: Salim completes the assignment unassisted and the launch-readiness checklist is green.
