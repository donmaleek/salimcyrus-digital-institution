## Problem Statement

Courses and masterclasses currently share the same catalog, content model, sales structure, and learning experience. Buyers have to understand the product taxonomy before they can decide what to buy. The business has no digital-learning demand evidence yet, while potential clients currently book private sessions for problems that structured teaching could address first.

The first target buyer is a woman facing relationship confusion who needs clarity before deciding whether to stay, leave, repair the relationship, or seek personal support.

## Demand Evidence

No direct demand evidence exists yet for paid digital learning. This launch is a paid demand test. Success must be measured from assessment starts through coaching conversion; page visits and compliments do not count as demand.

## Status Quo

The target buyer goes from free social content directly to a private session. This uses Salim's scarce time to explain repeatable frameworks and gives the buyer no lower-risk way to establish fit first.

## Target User and Narrowest Wedge

The narrowest wedge is one decision-intent pathway for women experiencing relationship uncertainty:

1. A free, private Relationship Clarity Assessment.
2. One recommended 60–90 minute `Relationship Reality Check` masterclass at KES 2,500.
3. One connected `Relationship Clarity Blueprint` course at KES 12,500.
4. A private Clarity Session only when the assessment or completed learning indicates that general education is insufficient.

No other assessment pathway or new digital product ships until this path produces paid evidence.

## Product Difference

### Masterclass: diagnose and decide

A masterclass addresses one urgent question in one sitting. It contains one focused video, a decision tool or worksheet, and a seven-day action plan. Its commercial job is to turn attention into a first purchase and help the buyer name the right next step. It does not promise full transformation.

### Course: learn, practice, and transform

A course installs a complete method through ordered modules, exercises, checkpoints, and repeated application. Its commercial job is to produce an observable change across several weeks and qualify the learner for coaching only where personal application remains blocked.

### Coaching: personalize and accelerate

Coaching applies the framework to facts the platform cannot safely interpret. It handles context, accountability, and personal decisions. Coaching is not presented as the automatic next purchase for every learner.

## Premises

1. Buyers understand their problem better than they understand the site's product categories.
2. Recommendations must explain why they were made and preserve alternative choices.
3. A masterclass must deliver a standalone result even when the buyer never upgrades.
4. A course must provide materially more than additional video duration.
5. Ethical clarity converts better over time than manufactured urgency in a vulnerable category.
6. The first launch should validate one connected ladder before the catalog expands.

## Market Evidence

Public offers support a ladder with distinct jobs: focused relationship masterclasses sell around $48–$99; deeper education adds tools, workshops, community, or coaching and reaches $199–$899. The opportunity is not to copy those prices. It is to make the transition between diagnosis, education, and personal help explicit, measurable, and appropriate for a Kenyan and international audience.

## Approaches Considered

- Simple Value Ladder: rejected because visitors would still need to choose the right format themselves.
- Transformation Ladder: rejected because upgrade credits alone do not solve product discovery.
- Clarity Pathway: approved because the assessment guides the buyer, generates demand evidence, and powers the full value ladder.

## Recommended Approach

### Customer journey

1. The academy entry point asks `What decision feels hardest right now?` and offers the assessment before either catalog.
2. The assessment asks one question per screen, supports back navigation, saves anonymous progress locally, and requests an account only when the visitor asks to save or view results.
3. The result names the buyer's current need, explains the recommendation, shows safety guidance where relevant, and recommends one masterclass.
4. After purchase, the learner sees a masterclass-specific player optimized for one sitting: agenda, worksheet, lesson, action plan, and completion reflection.
5. Completion issues a non-transferable KES 2,500 upgrade credit for the connected course, valid for seven calendar days. The sales page displays the original price, credit, payable balance, and expiry timestamp.
6. The course player uses modules, exercises, milestones, and resume state. Completion prompts a short outcome check.
7. Coaching is recommended only when the buyer selects personal help, reports an unresolved blocker, or meets admin-configured result criteria.

### Assessment model

The first assessment has six admin-managed questions. Each answer contributes integer points to one or more result keys. The highest score selects the recommendation; deterministic tie-breaking uses an admin-set priority. Results must never diagnose abuse, mental illness, or legal status.

Every result contains:

- A plain-language result name and explanation.
- `Why this result` with the buyer's relevant answers summarized without inventing facts.
- A recommended masterclass and optional alternatives.
- A non-commercial safety panel for answers indicating immediate danger, coercion, self-harm, or legal risk. In such cases, safety resources appear before any product offer.
- Consent controls for retaining assessment answers. Anonymous answers expire after 30 days; account-linked answers can be deleted from account settings.

### Product relationships

Admin can link a masterclass to one primary course and optionally one coaching offer. A course can receive referrals from multiple masterclasses. The relationship is versioned so changing a recommendation does not rewrite historical attribution.

### Upgrade credit rules

- Credit value equals the verified amount paid for the qualifying masterclass, capped at the connected course price.
- One credit per masterclass enrollment, one redemption only, tied to the same user account and currency.
- Credit starts when the masterclass is completed, not purchased, and expires after seven days.
- Refund, chargeback, rejected Paybill claim, or admin-revoked enrollment invalidates unused credit.
- Existing course owners cannot redeem a credit for cash or another product.
- Paystack and PayPal receive the discounted server-calculated amount and credit identifier in metadata. M-Pesa claims show the discounted amount to reviewers.
- Price and credit validation happen inside one database transaction when access is granted, preventing replay and double redemption.

### Admin experience

The course editor gains format-specific configuration rather than a cosmetic `kind` field.

For masterclasses, admin configures:

- One urgent question and one promised decision.
- Estimated session length, worksheet, and seven-day action plan.
- Connected course, upgrade-credit policy, and completion reflection.

For courses, admin configures:

- Transformation promise, estimated weeks, modules, exercises, and milestones.
- Prerequisites and linked masterclasses.
- Completion outcome check and coaching-fit criteria.

The admin dashboard adds:

- Assessment builder with question order, answer options, scores, result preview, and draft/published state.
- A path preview showing Assessment → Masterclass → Course → Coaching before publication.
- Publish validation that blocks missing recommendations, broken links, zero-outcome products, unsafe result copy, and invalid credit rules.
- Funnel analytics by assessment version, result, product, currency, and acquisition source.

### Catalog behavior

Catalogs remain available for returning buyers and direct links, but their roles become explicit:

- Masterclasses: `Solve one urgent relationship question today.`
- Courses: `Build a complete relationship decision and communication system.`

Cards use different evidence. Masterclasses show the question, minutes, tool, and immediate result. Courses show weeks, modules, exercises, milestones, and transformation outcome.

## Data and Service Boundaries

Extend `services/courses/` with versioned contracts for:

- Assessment, question, answer option, result, and response session.
- Product recommendation relationships.
- Upgrade-credit issuance, redemption, expiry, and invalidation.
- Funnel events and outcome checks.

Payment services consume a server-calculated checkout quote containing product price, credit, payable amount, currency, and expiry. They must not calculate discounts independently. Assessment scoring is a deterministic pure function with table-driven gate tests.

## Analytics and Measurable Outcome

Use first-party events with stable anonymous or account-linked journey IDs:

- `assessment_started`
- `assessment_answered`
- `assessment_completed`
- `recommendation_viewed`
- `masterclass_checkout_started`
- `masterclass_purchased`
- `masterclass_completed`
- `upgrade_credit_issued`
- `course_checkout_started`
- `upgrade_credit_redeemed`
- `course_completed`
- `coaching_requested`

Primary launch metric: at least 10 verified masterclass purchases from the first 200 completed assessments within 30 days, a 5% assessment-to-purchase rate.

Secondary metrics:

- At least 60% assessment completion.
- At least 50% purchased-masterclass completion.
- At least 15% of masterclass completers redeem the course credit.
- Refund and payment-dispute rate below 5%.
- At least five buyer interviews produce a repeated description of the result in the buyer's own words.

These are learning thresholds, not permanent targets. If fewer than 200 assessments complete, evaluate interviews and acquisition before judging offer demand.

## Safety, Trust, and Accessibility

- State that the assessment is educational and is not therapy, diagnosis, legal advice, or emergency support.
- Provide region-appropriate urgent-help guidance before commercial recommendations when a response indicates danger.
- Never tell a buyer to stay in or leave a relationship.
- Do not expose assessment answers in URLs, analytics payloads, email subjects, or application logs.
- Encrypt transport, restrict answer access to the member and authorized admins, and audit admin access.
- Support keyboard-only completion, screen readers, progress labels, back navigation, and reduced motion.
- Avoid countdown resets, false scarcity, hidden recurring charges, and preselected consent.

## Rollout

1. Internal dry run with every answer path and payment method.
2. Five moderated sessions where Salim silently observes target users complete the assessment and explain the result in their own words.
3. Private launch to an existing audience segment until 50 assessments complete.
4. Fix comprehension or trust failures before public promotion.
5. Public 30-day demand test. Do not add another pathway during the test.
6. Expand only if purchase, completion, interview, and refund evidence supports it.

## Tests and Evals

Gate tests under two seconds:

- Scoring, tie-breaking, version pinning, and incomplete-response rejection.
- Result safety override and absence of sensitive answers in URLs/log events.
- Recommendation relationship integrity and publish validation.
- Credit issuance, expiry boundary, currency isolation, replay prevention, refund invalidation, and concurrent redemption.
- Exact discounted amounts through Paystack, PayPal, and Paybill approval.
- Authorization for assessment responses and admin configuration.

Periodic evals:

- A buyer can distinguish a masterclass from a course without seeing the category label.
- Result explanations match supplied answers without diagnosis or invented claims.
- High-risk answers show help before a product.
- Admin can build, preview, publish, and analyze the complete path.
- Mobile and keyboard journeys reach checkout without dead ends.
- Funnel events reconstruct one journey without storing sensitive answer text.

## Open Questions

- Final wording and scoring for the six assessment questions require Salim's domain review.
- Safety resources must be selected for the countries the launch will target.
- The KES 2,500 and KES 12,500 prices are test prices, not proven willingness-to-pay.
- Refund handling must define whether redeemed credits reduce the refundable masterclass amount.

## Success Criteria

The feature is complete when an admin can publish a validated pathway, a visitor can receive a safe and explainable recommendation, all payment methods honor one exact credit-aware quote, the learner receives the correct experience for each format, and the dashboard reports the full assessment-to-coaching funnel without retaining sensitive answer text in analytics.

## The Assignment

Before implementation, interview five women who recently sought relationship clarity. Ask what decision they were trying to make, what they tried first, what they paid for, and what outcome would have justified KES 2,500. Do not pitch the proposed masterclass during the interview. Preserve their exact words and revise the first assessment and sales promise from repeated language.
